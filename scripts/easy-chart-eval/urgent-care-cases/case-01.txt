Thirty-four-year-old female here with two days of lower back pain that started after lifting a heavy box at work. Pain is across the lower back, does not shoot down the legs, no numbness or tingling, no weakness, no loss of bowel or bladder control, no saddle numbness. No fever, no recent trauma other than the lifting. On exam there is tenderness and muscle spasm of the lumbar paraspinal muscles, straight leg raise is negative bilaterally, strength is 5 out of 5 in the lower extremities, sensation intact, reflexes symmetric, normal gait. Diagnosis acute lumbar muscular strain without red flags. Discussed that imaging is not indicated at this stage, recommended staying active and avoiding bed rest, heat or ice, and a short course of ibuprofen and acetaminophen for pain. Reviewed proper lifting mechanics. Return precautions discussed including leg weakness, numbness in the groin area, loss of bladder or bowel control, fever, or pain that radiates down the leg, any of which would warrant prompt re-evaluation. Follow up with primary care in one to two weeks if not improving.
