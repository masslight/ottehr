Seven-year-old male here with two days of sore throat and fever to 102, says it hurts to swallow and he has not been eating much. No cough, no runny nose, no hoarseness. One of his classmates was recently diagnosed with strep. No rash noted by mom. Up-to-date on vaccines and otherwise healthy. On exam tonsils are enlarged and erythematous with white exudate, tender anterior cervical lymph nodes bilaterally, no drooling and no trismus. Rapid strep antigen was positive. Diagnosis acute streptococcal pharyngitis. Will start amoxicillin 50 mg per kilogram once daily times ten days. Advised he is no longer contagious after 24 hours on antibiotics and can return to school at that time. Can continue ibuprofen and acetaminophen alternating for pain and fever. Encouraged fluids and soft foods. Return precautions discussed including difficulty breathing, inability to swallow secretions, drooling, neck swelling or stiffness, or any significant worsening. Follow up with pediatrician if not improving in two to three days.
