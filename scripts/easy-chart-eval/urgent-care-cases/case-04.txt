Two-year-old here with a barky seal-like cough and noisy breathing that started last night and is worse with crying. Low-grade fever to 100.8, runny nose for a couple of days prior. No drooling, taking fluids, making wet diapers. No prior intubations and no underlying lung disease. On exam there is intermittent inspiratory stridor when upset that resolves when calm, mild suprasternal retractions, lungs otherwise clear with good air movement, no signs of respiratory distress at rest. Diagnosis is viral croup. Gave a single dose of dexamethasone 0.6 mg per kilogram by mouth in the clinic which he tolerated well. Discussed cool mist and going outside into cool air if stridor recurs. Return precautions discussed including stridor at rest, retractions, drooling, blue color around the lips, or any significant worsening of breathing — advised to seek emergency care for those. Follow up with pediatrician in one to two days. Reassured parents that croup symptoms typically peak over two to three nights and then improve.
