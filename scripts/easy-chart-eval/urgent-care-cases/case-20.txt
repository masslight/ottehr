Three-year-old here brought by dad for foul-smelling discharge from the left nostril for two days, only on the one side. No fever, eating and drinking normally. Dad is not aware of anything being put up the nose but the child plays with small beads. On exam there is purulent discharge from the left nostril, and on examination a small bead-like foreign body is visualized in the left nasal passage, the right side is clear, no respiratory distress. Performed positive-pressure technique with the parent giving a quick puff of air into the mouth while occluding the unaffected nostril, and the foreign body was expelled successfully. Re-examined and the nasal passage is now clear with no remaining objects and only minor mucosal irritation. Diagnosis nasal foreign body, now removed. Discussed nasal saline for comfort and that the discharge should resolve over the next day or two. Counseled about keeping small objects out of reach. Return precautions discussed including recurrent one-sided discharge, bleeding, fever, or any concern that another object remains. Follow up with pediatrician as needed.
