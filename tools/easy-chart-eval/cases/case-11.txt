Forty-one-year-old male here with two days of redness, warmth, and swelling of the right lower leg that has been spreading, started after he scratched the area in the yard. Reports a low-grade fever and that the area is tender. No drainage, no recent travel, no animal bites. On exam there is an area of confluent erythema over the anterior right shin measuring roughly eight by six centimeters, warm and tender to palpation with poorly defined but advancing borders which I outlined with a skin marker, no fluctuance, no pus, no crepitus, no red streaking proximally, intact distal pulses and sensation. Diagnosis cellulitis without abscess. Started cephalexin four times daily times seven days. Discussed elevating the leg, and that the marked border lets us track whether the redness advances. Return precautions discussed including redness spreading beyond the marked line, increasing pain or swelling, fluctuance or pus, red streaking, high fever, or feeling generally unwell, any of which would warrant prompt re-evaluation and possible intravenous antibiotics. Follow up in two to three days for a recheck.
