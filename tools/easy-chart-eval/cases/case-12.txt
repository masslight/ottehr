Three-year-old male here with one day of vomiting and watery diarrhea, mom counts roughly six episodes of each. Tolerating small sips now without vomiting in the last two hours. Decreased urine output, last wet diaper about six hours ago. No blood in the stool, no significant abdominal pain, low-grade fever. Several family members ill with a stomach bug. On exam he is tired-appearing but consolable, mucous membranes slightly dry, capillary refill less than two seconds, abdomen soft and non-tender with normal bowel sounds, no rebound or guarding. Diagnosis viral gastroenteritis with mild dehydration. Gave a dose of ondansetron 0.15 mg per kilogram in the clinic and started a supervised oral rehydration trial with small frequent sips which he tolerated. Discussed continuing small amounts of fluid frequently rather than large volumes, and resuming a regular diet as tolerated. Return precautions discussed including no urine in eight hours, no tears, persistent vomiting, bloody stools, severe abdominal pain, or lethargy. Follow up with pediatrician in one to two days or sooner if worsening.
