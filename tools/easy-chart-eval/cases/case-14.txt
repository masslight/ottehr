Eighteen-month-old here with two days of fever to 101 and now fussiness and decreased oral intake, mom noticed sores in his mouth and a rash on his hands. Several kids at daycare reportedly have a similar illness. Still making some wet diapers but drinking less than usual. On exam there are several shallow ulcers on the buccal mucosa and tongue, small red macules and a few vesicles on the palms and soles, and a few spots on the buttocks, he is fussy but consolable and well-hydrated with moist mucous membranes. Diagnosis is hand-foot-and-mouth disease, a viral illness. Discussed that this is self-limited and supportive care is the mainstay, push cold fluids, popsicles, and acetaminophen or ibuprofen for pain and fever. Reviewed that it is contagious and good hand hygiene is important. Return precautions discussed including signs of dehydration such as no wet diaper in eight hours, no tears, dry mouth, lethargy, or refusing all fluids. Follow up with pediatrician if not improving in a few days.
