Forty-eight-year-old male here with a painful red bump on the right buttock that has been growing over four days and now has a white head and is very tender. No fever, no diabetes, no immune problems. On exam there is a three centimeter fluctuant abscess with surrounding erythema and central pointing, warm and tender, no extensive surrounding cellulitis and no crepitus. After informed consent, the area was anesthetized with lidocaine and an incision and drainage was performed, expressing a moderate amount of purulent material, the cavity was irrigated and loosely packed with gauze. A wound culture was sent given the possibility of MRSA. Diagnosis cutaneous abscess, drained. Started trimethoprim-sulfamethoxazole twice daily times seven days. Discussed warm compresses, keeping the area clean, and that the packing should be removed and the wound rechecked in two days. Ibuprofen as needed for pain. Return precautions discussed including spreading redness, increasing pain or swelling, fever, or red streaking. Follow up here in two days for packing removal and a wound check.
