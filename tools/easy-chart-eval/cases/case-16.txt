Fourteen-year-old male here after hitting heads with another player during a soccer game about three hours ago, had a brief moment of feeling dazed and confused but no loss of consciousness per teammates. Now has a headache, feels foggy, and is bothered by bright lights. No vomiting, no neck pain, no weakness or numbness, no seizure. On exam he is alert and oriented, neuro exam is normal with intact cranial nerves, normal strength, sensation, coordination, and gait, no neck tenderness, no focal deficits, no signs of skull fracture. Diagnosis concussion. Discussed cognitive and physical rest for the next 24 to 48 hours with a gradual return to activities as symptoms allow, and a stepwise return-to-play protocol only after he is symptom-free and cleared. He should not return to sports until evaluated. Reviewed limiting screen time. Return precautions discussed including worsening or severe headache, repeated vomiting, increasing confusion or drowsiness, slurred speech, weakness, numbness, seizure, or difficulty waking — advised to go to the emergency department for any of these. Follow up with pediatrician in two to three days.
