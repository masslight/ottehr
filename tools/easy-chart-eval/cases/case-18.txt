Six-year-old here with one day of a red right eye with yellow discharge, mom reports the lashes were crusted shut this morning. Mild irritation, no eye pain, no vision change, no photophobia. No contact lens use. A classmate has had pink eye. On exam there is conjunctival injection of the right eye with mucopurulent discharge, no corneal opacity, pupils equal and reactive, no proptosis or periorbital swelling, vision grossly intact. Diagnosis bacterial conjunctivitis. Started erythromycin ophthalmic ointment to the affected eye times five days. Discussed frequent hand washing, not sharing towels or pillows, and that the discharge is contagious, may return to school after 24 hours of treatment. Advised warm compresses to remove crusting. Return precautions discussed including eye pain, vision changes, light sensitivity, swelling or redness of the eyelid or around the eye, which would warrant re-evaluation. Follow up with pediatrician if not improving in two to three days.
